In any democracy, the power truly lies with the people. Citizens have the responsibility to hold their leaders accountable and ensure they fulfil their promises. However, to do this effectively, you must first know who your representatives are. This knowledge is the foundation of active citizenship.

##  Who Are Your Representatives?
Your representatives are the people elected to serve you at different levels of government. In Nigeria, these include:-
- **The President** at the federal level.
- **The Governor** at the state level
- **Members of the House of Assembly** at the state level.
- **Senators and Members of the House of Representatives** at the federal level.
- **Local Government Chairman** at the local government level.

Each of these individuals plays a specific role in making decisions that affect your community, state, and the entire country.

## Why Is It Important to Know Them?
1. **Accountability:** When you know your representatives, it becomes easier to monitor their performance. Are they delivering on their campaign promises? Are they passing laws and policies that benefit the people?.
1. **Access:** Knowing who represents you allows you to reach out to them with concerns, suggestions, or complaints. Platforms like shineyoureye.org make this even easier by providing contact details for elected officials.
1. **Advocacy:** Understanding who makes decisions about issues you care about helps you focus your advocacy efforts. For example, if you want better schools in your area, knowing your Local Government Chairman  or state representative responsible can guide your actions.
1. **Informed Voting:** When you know your representatives, you’re better equipped to decide whether they deserve your vote in the next election. This ensures only capable leaders remain in office.


## How to Find Your Representatives
It’s easier than ever to know your representatives. Platforms like shineyoureye.org provide detailed information about elected officials, including their names, roles, and contact information.
You can also:
- Visit your local government office for details.
- Follow news updates about your area’s leaders.
- Engage in community meetings or forums where representatives often speak.


## What Can You Do After Knowing Them?
1. **Contact Them:** Share your concerns and ideas. Let them know what matters to you and your community.
1. **Monitor Their Work:** Keep track of the bills they support, the projects they execute, and how they use public funds.
1. **Hold Them Accountable:** If they fail to perform, speak up through peaceful protests, petitions, or by voting them out in the next election.
1. **Support Good Leaders:** When representatives do well, support and encourage them. Positive feedback motivates them to do more.


Active citizenship starts with knowing your representatives. By understanding who they are and what they do, you can engage with them more effectively and play your part in building a better Nigeria. Democracy works best when citizens are informed, involved, and empowered to act. Start today by finding out who represents you and how you can reach them.
